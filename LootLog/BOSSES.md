# Boss list for special first-kill messages

286 bosses from the creature list (everything with designation boss, which includes every creature with level ??).
Each one already gets the general boss message the first time you kill it. To give a boss messages of its own, send
a line such as:

    Hogger | Hogger is down. Somewhere, a gnoll sheds a tear. | Another message for variety

(name or ID, then one or more messages separated by |). Use {name} where the boss's name should appear.
The messages go into `BossData.lua`. Tick the box here when a boss has its own messages.
